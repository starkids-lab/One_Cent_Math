AP Precalculus

A structured AP Precalculus pathway centered on modeling, functions, multiple representations, symbolic fluency, communication, and reasoning. It follows the current College Board AP Precalculus framework while making the Georgia connection explicit.

COURSE MAP

Learn the ideas that connect.

Each module is designed to move from concept → representation → practice → application. Use the map as a course outline or jump directly to a topic.

01

Unit 1 · Polynomial & Rational Functions

Rates of change, polynomial behavior, zeros, rational functions, holes, asymptotes, modeling, assumptions, and limitations.

02

Unit 2 · Exponential & Logarithmic Functions

Geometric sequences, exponential models, inverses, logarithms, composition, and residual-based model validation.

03

Unit 3 · Trigonometric & Polar Functions

Radians, trig functions, sinusoidal modeling, inverse trig, polar coordinates, graphs, and periodic phenomena.

04

Unit 4 · Parameters, Vectors & Matrices

Parametric functions, conics, vectors, transformation matrices, and contextual modeling. This unit is not assessed on the AP exam but is part of the framework.

05

Practice 1 · Symbolic Fluency

Manipulate functions, equations, and expressions accurately and efficiently.

06

Practice 2 · Multiple Representations

Translate between graphs, tables, equations, diagrams, descriptions, and technology outputs.

07

Practice 3 · Communication & Reasoning

Explain conclusions, justify choices, identify assumptions, and communicate mathematical reasoning precisely.

08

Modeling Studio

Build and defend function models for authentic scenarios, compare models, interpret parameters, and discuss limitations.

Standards & alignment

College Board’s current AP Precalculus framework is organized into four units: Polynomial and Rational Functions; Exponential and Logarithmic Functions; Trigonometric and Polar Functions; and Functions Involving Parameters, Vectors, and Matrices. The first three are assessed on the AP Exam; Unit 4 is not. College Board also emphasizes three mathematical practices: Procedural & Symbolic Fluency, Multiple Representations, and Communication & Reasoning. Georgia’s Precalculus and accelerated Enhanced Advanced Algebra & AP Precalculus pathways can be used alongside this framework.
