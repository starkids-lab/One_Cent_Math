About One Cent Math

One Cent Math exists for a simple reason: high-quality mathematics education should not be locked behind paywalls, accounts, or restricted access.

This site provides a complete, open Precalculus course built directly on the Georgia Precalculus (PC) standards. Every note, every worksheet, and every solution is free for anyone to use, adapt, and share.

What makes it different

  • Fully open — no sign-in, no premium tiers, no teacher-only keys.
  • Comprehensive — deeper explanations and more practice than typical free resources.
  • Original structure — designed for logical progression and lasting understanding, not just topic coverage.
  • Ready to expand — Calculus will be added later using the same clean framework.
  • Yours to own — download the entire project and edit everything yourself.

The name

“One Cent” reflects the idea that great math education should cost almost nothing — or nothing at all. Knowledge this fundamental should be accessible to every student.

You own this site

All source files are yours. Host them anywhere, change the design, replace the sample content with your own notes and videos, add new units, or rebrand it. There are no restrictions.
