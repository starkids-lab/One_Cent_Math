COURSE MAP

Learn the ideas that connect.

Each module is designed to move from concept → representation → practice → application. Use the map as a course outline or jump directly to a topic.

01

Polynomial Structure

Polynomial operations, identities, complex numbers, zeros, factors, end behavior, and polynomial models.

02

Rational Expressions & Functions

Equivalent forms, operations, restrictions, graphs, asymptotes, equations, extraneous solutions, and applications.

03

Exponential & Logarithmic Functions

Inverse relationships, laws of logarithms, growth/decay models, equations, and interpreting parameters.

04

Radical & Power Functions

Rational exponents, radical equations, transformations, domains, and modeling with power functions.

05

Trigonometric Foundations

Radians, unit-circle values, graphs, identities, inverse trig functions, and equation solving.

06

Matrices & Linear Programming

Matrix operations, systems, inverse matrices, constraints, feasible regions, and optimization.

07

Data & Statistical Reasoning

Collecting, critiquing, analyzing, and interpreting data; association, models, and statistical communication.

08

Sequences & Series

Recursive and explicit sequences, arithmetic/geometric patterns, series, finite sums, and applications.

09

Modeling & Function Families

Select, transform, compare, and validate function models across algebraic and real-world contexts.

10

Advanced Capstone

Combine functions, algebra, trigonometry, matrices, data, and modeling in a multi-step investigation.

Standards & alignment

Georgia identifies Advanced Algebra: Concepts & Connections with AA.MP practices and content areas including AA.PAR, AA.FGR, AA.DSR and related strands. Georgia also offers an Enhanced Advanced Algebra & AP Precalculus pathway that thoughtfully blends Advanced Algebra with Precalculus. This page uses that accelerated pathway as its organizing idea while keeping the topics useful beyond Georgia.
